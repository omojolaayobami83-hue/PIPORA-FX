PIPORA FX Privacy Notice

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This notice describes the current public prototype of PIPORA FX. It should be reviewed and updated before the app is used commercially or new data collection features are added.

Information you enter

The current app does not provide user accounts or cloud profiles. Trade-journal entries are stored in local browser storage on the device and browser where you use the app. They are not uploaded to a PIPORA account. Clearing browser data or changing devices may remove access to those entries. Avoid entering passwords, bank details or other sensitive information in journal notes.

Market data and third-party services

Market-price requests are sent by the PIPORA server to Twelve Data using a private server-side API key. The app also loads chart content from TradingView. Those providers may process technical request information under their own privacy terms. PIPORA does not control their independent data practices.

Local storage and offline support

The app uses browser storage for the journal and a service worker cache for basic app files. Cached files do not include live market data. The app does not currently advertise or use an analytics or advertising tracker.

Hosting and security

The hosting provider may process connection and server-log information to operate and protect the service. No internet service can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not treat the app as a secure document vault.

Children and changes

This tool is intended for adults who understand the risks of leveraged trading. This notice may change as features and providers change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

A public support contact has not yet been configured. A contact channel should be added before broad public release.
